My Unofficial Guide to Final Destination Movies

What is Final Destination?

Final Destination is a movie franchise (currently 6 movies) answering a hypothetical question of what happens when you escape death.


The first movie came out in 2000 and defined it as a supernatural slasher series. The antagonist is the external force of death itself which hunts down people who cheat the already established. Those who escape death in fatal accidents are now in a race against death to find the signals and avoid the peril before it comes back to bite them.
